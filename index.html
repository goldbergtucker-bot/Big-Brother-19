BB19 Twists

The simulator uses the BB19 temptation era and custom strategic systems.

$25,000 Garden of Temptation The first 16 houseguests begin the game. The $25,000 is always taken; the first of those 16 to press wins the money, then the editable 17th houseguest enters the game and receives the Friendship Bracelet power.
Friendship Bracelets & Hit the Road The 17th houseguest gives Friendship Bracelets to exactly 8 of the original 16. The other 8 compete in Hit the Road; the winner is safe and exactly 3 houseguests become the opening nominees. The 17th houseguest can vote in the three-nominee eviction.
Week 1 Self-Eviction After the first HOH's nominations, one nominee is guaranteed to self-evict. A replacement nominee is named, and the self-eviction does not count as a normal eviction, placement or jury member.
Den of Temptation Pendant of Protection, Ring of Replacement and Halting Hex.
Temptation Competition Weeks 5–7: optional competition for immunity, with the last-place finisher becoming a third nominee.
Battle Back Showdown The first four evicted houseguests compete for a chance to return.
Tree of Temptation Weeks 8–10: secret powers and punishments selected from the apple tree.
Double Eviction Fast-paced second eviction cycle during the late game.
